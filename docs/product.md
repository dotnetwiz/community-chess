# Product

## Confirmed

- Name: Community Chess.
- The game uses standard chess rules on each board.
- The initial match runs three chess games in parallel, with three players on each team.
- The match uses timed play to identify when a player is taking too long or has left; exact timing rules remain undecided.
- On each team turn, all three teammates are assigned to different active boards and may make their moves simultaneously.
- Once all assigned moves are made, the opposing team takes its turn on the active boards.
- Player-to-board assignments rotate between turns so each player cycles through each active board.
- A player may view and move only on the board currently assigned to them. After completing their move, they cannot view that board again until their rotation returns to it.
- Players receive notifications when one of their turns is ready on a board.
- When a player times out or bows out, they are removed from the match and their seat opens for another player to join.
- When a seat opens, the team may wait for a replacement or forfeit the affected board and continue on the other two boards with two players.
- If the team waits for a replacement and no one joins before a further timeout, the affected board is forfeited and play continues on the remaining boards.
- The team that wins at least two of the three boards wins the match.

- Draw handling and match scoring when fewer than two boards are won.
