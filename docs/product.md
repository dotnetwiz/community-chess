# Product

## Confirmed

- Name: Community Chess
- The game uses standard chess rules.
- The game is played by multiple players on each side.
- Teammates take turns moving for their side.
- Teammates may not communicate or coordinate.

## Deliver Next

- A single-board 3v3 game.
- Players move in a defined team rotation.
- No in-game teammate communication.

## Deliver Later

- A three-board 3v3 relay match.
- Player order staggered across boards so teammates take different positions in the rotation.
- Match scoring across the three boards.

## To Decide
